Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 4, 2026

Information we use

When you sign in, Firebase Authentication provides your name, email address, profile image, and account identifier. We use this information to create your account, secure access, and associate bookings with you.

Bookings and payments

Event and booking details are stored to provide tickets and organizer features. Payment details are sent to the selected payment provider. EventBooking does not store card numbers, PayPal passwords, or M-Pesa PINs.

Service providers

Firebase, the configured email provider, PayPal, and Safaricom M-Pesa process information needed for authentication, notifications, and payment confirmation. Their own privacy policies also apply.

Retention and choices

We retain account and booking records for service, accounting, and support needs. Contact the service administrator to request access, correction, or deletion where applicable.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the service administrator through the address associated with this deployment.
